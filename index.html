02 Editor's Letter
Communications & New Media Statement

Where Communication, Digital Media & Human-Centred Technology Meet

Why Communications and New Media is the natural home for a Literature student who leads public relations teams, builds data-driven brand listening workflows, and co-designs educational software.

TODO: Draft Editor's Letter — to be rewritten by Bao Anh

Studying in the Literature specialization class at Hanoi–Amsterdam High School for the Gifted has trained me to look closely at how language, media, and narrative shape community trust. Across the student organisations I help lead—as Head of Public Relations for Science Alive, Vice Head of Public Relations for RICEPsy, Media Advisor for Scribbles, and PR member for Society of Open Science, Science Tornado, and Science Fair—my work centres on translating scientific and psychological ideas into public-facing campaigns that resonate with young audiences.

In summer 2026, I brought this approach into industry as a Brand Communication Intern at Staaar Joint Stock Company (TokyoLife brand), combining communication management with data-driven new media by collecting and classifying 30 days of social media customer interactions using local AI tools and Apify web scraping, designing a real-time sentiment analytics dashboard, and translating those insights into store layout and brand concept adjustments.

That same interest in how people connect across digital systems guided two distinct co-invention projects: iTutorTDN, an e-learning assistant program awarded a Silver Medal at KIDE 2021 and granted Vietnamese Utility Solution Patent No. 4647, and iViHRM, an AI-powered human resource management system for SMEs recognised with a Gold Medal at KIDE 2025 and the MIIA Leading Innovation Award.
